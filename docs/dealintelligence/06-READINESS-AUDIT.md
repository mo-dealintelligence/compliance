## What "day-to-day" looks like once B1–B8 are closed

- Quarterly: scheduled Chrome run captures the shot list and uploads; task-schedule flips expired tasks to `todo` and emails you; board/IT meeting minutes go in as `board-meeting` / `it-leadership-meeting` forms instead of PDFs.
- Yearly: policy review dates → `needs_review`; re-publish → re-acknowledgment in the portal; performance evaluation and tabletop exercise as forms; training re-completion.
- Continuous: access requests, whistleblower reports and findings have their own forms; new vendors/risks created in-app; auditor gets an `auditor` role invite (read-only) when the Type II window starts.
