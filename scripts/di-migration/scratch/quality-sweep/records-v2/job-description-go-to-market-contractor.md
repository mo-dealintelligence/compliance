# Job Description - Go-to-Market Contractor

## 1. Position summary

The Go-to-Market Contractor is responsible for customer acquisition, sales and marketing, customer support, and market intelligence for Deal Intelligence LLC's B2B market intelligence platform. The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) retains all technical, infrastructure, security, and compliance responsibilities.

The role operates within Deal Intelligence's SOC 2 compliance program and is bound by the Information Security Policy, the Acceptable Use Policy, and the Code of Conduct.

## 2. Access level

The Go-to-Market Contractor has no production access: no AWS, GitHub, DocumentDB, or PropelAuth administration. Access is limited to:

| System | Access |
|---|---|
| Google Workspace (dealintelligence.io) | Personnel account with MFA |
| Compliance platform portal | Policy acknowledgment and security training |
| Business tools approved by the CEO | Standard user |

## 3. Responsibilities

### 3.1 Go-to-market strategy (25 %)

Market strategy:
- Develop and execute the go-to-market strategy for the platform.
- Identify target markets and customer segments.
- Define value propositions and messaging aligned with platform capabilities.
- Create competitive positioning.
- Develop pricing strategy with the CEO.

Marketing planning:
- Create marketing plans and campaigns.
- Develop the content strategy.
- Plan product launches and feature announcements.
- Coordinate marketing activities.
- Track marketing metrics and report on campaign performance.

### 3.2 Sales and business development (35 %)

Lead generation:
- Identify and qualify leads within target segments.
- Build prospect lists using approved tools and methods.
- Conduct outbound outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone).
- Attend networking events (virtual and in person).
- Generate referrals from existing customer relationships.

Sales process:
- Conduct product demonstrations.
- Present value propositions tailored to prospect needs.
- Handle objections and customer concerns.
- Negotiate terms and pricing within approved parameters.
- Close deals in accordance with company policies.
- Manage the sales pipeline and provide forecasts.

Account management:
- Onboard new customers following established procedures.
- Manage ongoing customer relationships.
- Identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities.
- Conduct quarterly business reviews.
- Monitor customer success and satisfaction.

### 3.3 Marketing and content (20 %)

Content creation:
- Write blog posts, articles, and thought-leadership content.
- Develop case studies and customer success stories.
- Create sales collateral and presentation materials.
- Produce social media content.
- Draft email marketing campaigns.

Digital marketing:
- Manage the social media presence on approved platforms.
- Coordinate non-technical website content updates.
- Support SEO content initiatives.
- Track and analyze marketing performance metrics.

### 3.4 Customer support and success (15 %)

Customer communication:
- Act as primary point of contact for customer inquiries.
- Respond to customer questions by email and scheduled calls.
- Escalate technical issues to the CEO promptly.
- Document customer feedback and feature requests.

Customer success:
- Monitor customer engagement and platform adoption.
- Identify at-risk accounts early.
- Develop and execute customer retention actions.
- Gather testimonials and referrals.

### 3.5 Market intelligence (5 %)

- Monitor competitor activities and market trends.
- Gather and document competitive intelligence.
- Report market insights to inform product development.
- Track industry developments relevant to B2B intelligence.

## 4. Security and compliance responsibilities

### 4.1 Data handling

- Handle all customer and prospect data per the Data Classification and Handling Standard.
- Protect business contact information (names, emails, LinkedIn profiles, job titles, company information) as Confidential data.
- Follow the Data Retention and Disposal Policy for all customer records.
- Never share customer data with unauthorized parties or use personal accounts for business communications.

### 4.2 Access and authentication

- Keep unique login credentials; never share account access.
- Use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all business systems.
- Report suspected unauthorized access immediately to the CEO.
- Comply with session timeout and secure logout requirements.
- Follow the Access Control and Authentication Policy.

### 4.3 Acceptable use

- Use company-approved tools and platforms for all business activities.
- Follow the Acceptable Use Policy for all system access.
- Do not install unauthorized software or browser extensions.
- Access company systems only through browser single sign-on (Google Workspace) with MFA.

### 4.4 Incident reporting

- Report suspected security incidents, data breaches, or policy violations immediately to the CEO.
- Record incidents in the compliance platform.
- Cooperate fully with incident investigations.
- Follow the Incident Response Procedure.

### 4.5 Privacy and data protection

- Escalate all data subject requests (access, deletion, correction) to the CEO per the Data Subject Rights Request Procedure.
- Maintain awareness of GDPR and CCPA requirements for B2B contact data.
- Process personal data only within the scope of assigned responsibilities.

### 4.6 Training

- Complete security awareness training in the compliance platform portal at onboarding and annually. [CONFIRM: v1 read "within 5 business days of contract start"; R4 replaces the 5-day rule]
- Acknowledge the Code of Conduct annually.
- Complete role-specific training as assigned.
- Requirements are in the Security Awareness Training Program.

## 5. Boundaries

### 5.1 Activities outside scope

The following activities are the exclusive responsibility of the CEO and are not within the scope of this role:

| Category | Excluded activities |
|---|---|
| Technical operations | System administration, infrastructure management, code deployment, database access |
| Security administration | Access provisioning, security configuration, vulnerability management, penetration testing |
| Compliance management | Policy creation or modification, audit responses, compliance attestations, risk assessments |
| Data processing | Direct database queries, bulk data exports, data migration, backup management |
| Vendor management | Signing vendor agreements, security assessments, sub-processor approvals |
| Financial systems | Payment processing configuration, billing system administration |

### 5.2 Escalation to the CEO

- Any customer request for data export, deletion, or modification.
- Technical issues or platform bugs reported by customers.
- Security concerns or suspected incidents.
- Requests for custom integrations or API access.
- Contract terms outside standard parameters.
- Any request that may affect compliance obligations.

## 6. Required competence

Experience:
- 3+ years of B2B sales or business development experience.
- Success in SaaS or technology sales.
- Experience with CRM systems and sales tools.
- Record of meeting or exceeding sales targets.

Skills:
- Written and verbal communication.
- Presentation and demonstration.
- Proficiency with the sales and marketing technology stack.
- Ability to understand and articulate technical product value.
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

Knowledge:
- B2B sales cycles and enterprise buying processes.
- Market intelligence or data products (preferred).
- Data privacy concepts (GDPR and CCPA awareness).
- Professional networking and lead generation practices.

## 7. Performance metrics

| Category | Key performance indicators |
|---|---|

| Customer success | Customer satisfaction scores, retention rate, expansion revenue |
| Marketing | Content production, engagement metrics, lead quality |
| Compliance | Training completion, policy acknowledgments, incident reporting timeliness |

## 8. Working arrangements

Remote work:
- Maintain a secure home office environment.
- Access company systems only through browser single sign-on (Google Workspace) with MFA. [CONFIRM: v1 read "encrypted connections (VPN when required)"; R4 removes VPN]
- Maintain physical security of any device used for work.
- Follow the remote work requirements in the Acceptable Use Policy.

Communication and availability:
- Maintain regular communication with the CEO.
- Respond to customer inquiries within agreed service levels.
- Attend scheduled check-ins with the CEO.
- Document activities and keep accurate records in approved systems.
