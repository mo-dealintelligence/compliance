# Ethics Violation Log - 2025

## 1. Purpose

This log records all reported ethics violations, investigations, and resolutions for calendar year 2025. It is the evidence record for SOC 2 criterion CC1.1 (integrity and ethical values) and supports the Code of Conduct.
