# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards and behavioral expectations for all personnel, contractors, and stakeholders of Deal Intelligence. It ensures alignment with our SOC 2 compliance objectives and supports our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of integrity in providing B2B market intelligence services.

## Scope

This Code applies to all Deal Intelligence personnel, including:

- The Chief Executive Officer

- The Go-to-Market Contractor

- Contractors and consultants engaged by Deal Intelligence

- Third-party representatives acting on behalf of Deal Intelligence

## Core Principles

1. **Integrity:** Act honestly and ethically in all business dealings, maintaining transparency in our AI-powered market intelligence operations

2. **Respect:** Treat all individuals with dignity and respect, fostering an inclusive environment

3. **Compliance:** Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies, including SOC 2, GDPR, and CCPA requirements

4. **Confidentiality:** Protect sensitive information and customer data in accordance with our Data Classification and Handling Standard

5. **Accountability:** Take responsibility for actions and decisions, maintaining accurate records and audit trails

## Professional Conduct

### General Standards

- Maintain high standards of professional behavior in all interactions

- Represent Deal Intelligence accurately and honestly

- Use company resources responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes

- Maintain accurate records and documentation

- Report violations of this code through appropriate channels

### Conflicts of Interest

- Disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest to the CEO

- Avoid situations where personal interests could compromise professional judgment

- Do not accept gifts or benefits that could influence business decisions

- Obtain approval from the CEO before engaging in outside employment or business activities

### Intellectual Property

- Respect intellectual property rights of Deal Intelligence and third parties

- Protect proprietary algorithms and market intelligence methodologies

- Follow guidelines in our Acceptable Use Policy for system and data usage

- Ensure proper licensing for all software and tools used

## Data Protection and Privacy

### Customer Data Handling

- Handle business contact information and market intelligence data with appropriate care

- Follow security protocols established in our Information Security Policy

- Access only data necessary for job functions following principle of least privilege

- Comply with retention periods defined in our Data Retention and Disposal Policy

### Security Responsibilities

- Report security incidents immediately using the compliance platform Incident Reporting system

- Follow Access Control and Authentication Policy requirements including MFA usage

- Protect authentication credentials and never share access

- Complete mandatory Security Awareness Training Program requirements

### Privacy Compliance

- Respect data subject rights under GDPR and CCPA

- Follow procedures outlined in our Data Subject Rights Request Procedure

- Ensure appropriate consent and legal basis for data processing

- Maintain transparency in data collection and usage practices

## Technology and Systems Use

### Acceptable Use

- Use Deal Intelligence systems and AWS infrastructure responsibly

- Follow secure development practices per our Secure Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) Standard

- Comply with API Security and Rate Limiting Standard requirements

- Adhere to Change Management Policy for system modifications

### Third-Party Interactions

- Follow the Integration security section of the API Security and Rate Limiting Standard when implementing integrations

- Ensure vendors comply with our Vendor Management Policy

- Protect credentials and API keys for external services

- Document all third-party dependencies and integrations

## Reporting and Non-Retaliation

### Reporting Obligations

All personnel must report:

- Violations of this Code of Conduct

- Security incidents or data breaches

- Suspected illegal or unethical behavior

- Conflicts of interest or potential compliance issues

Reports should be made through:

- The CEO

- The compliance platform Incident Reporting system for security incidents

- Anonymous reporting channels when available

### Non-Retaliation Policy

Deal Intelligence prohibits retaliation against anyone who:

- Reports violations in good faith

- Participates in investigations

- Raises compliance concerns

- Refuses to participate in activities violating this Code

## Compliance and Enforcement

### Acknowledgment

- All personnel must acknowledge understanding of this Code at onboarding

- Annual re-acknowledgment is required and recorded in the compliance platform portal

- Training completion must be documented in the compliance platform

### Disciplinary Actions

Violations of this Code may result in:

- Verbal or written warnings

- Performance improvement plans

- Suspension with or without pay

- Termination of employment or contract

- Legal action when appropriate

### Monitoring and Review

- Compliance is monitored through regular audits and assessments

- This Code is reviewed annually and updated as needed

- Effectiveness is measured through compliance metrics and incident tracking

## Related Policies and Procedures

This Code of Conduct should be read in conjunction with:

- Information Security Policy

- Acceptable Use Policy

- Incident Response Procedure

- Privacy Notice and Data Processing Agreement Template

## Acknowledgment

By acknowledging electronically in the compliance platform portal, I confirm that I have read, understood, and agree to comply with Deal Intelligence's Code of Conduct.
